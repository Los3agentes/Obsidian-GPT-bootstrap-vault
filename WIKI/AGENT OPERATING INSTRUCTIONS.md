# Agent Operating Instructions

Before work: identify objective, authorized root, applicable AGENTS files, and active skill.

During work: inspect files, plan minimal changes, preserve signatures and headers, avoid secret exposure, and maintain change traceability.

After work: reread outputs, check syntax and paths, update CHANGELOG, and report precisely what was and was not completed.

Agent Bond: agents share consistent conventions and auditability, but never assume permissions transfer across agents or environments.
